FAQ

The questions people ask us.

Is Kimpay really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no hidden premium tier.

Do I need an account?

No. A link is all it takes to join a group. An account (a magic link sent by email) only helps you find your groups on several devices.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Kimpay installs like an app: offline, you can still see your groups and add expenses, and everything syncs once you are back online.

How are the transfers calculated?

Kimpay looks for the smallest possible number of transfers and shares out the rounding cents: the total always adds up.
